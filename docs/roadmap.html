Transport & Co. Roadmap

Full check of the playable build on 28 October 2026, and the 32 updates that came out of it. All six phases are built, tested and live in the game, and so are the six things that were still missing after that.

32 updates shipped 6 limits lifted 32 updates left 96 automated checks passing

Working now (tested)

Start menu and random maps4 climates, difficulty, size, towns, rivals, start year, seed. Same seed gives the same map; towns all differ; slopes stay smooth.
BuildingDrag roads and track, bus and truck stops, train stations, both depots, bridges and tunnels, raise and lower land, bulldoze.
Buses and trucks9 buses, 18 trucks. A truck for every cargo from 1950. They load, deliver and get paid.
RailwaysTrack, stations, train depots. Locomotive plus 1 to 20 wagons, a wagon for each of the 7 materials in every era.
Industry chainsCoal to power station or steel mill, timber to sawmill, grain to factory, steel to factory, goods to towns. Every link tested.
Orders and fleetFleet list, full load or load available, deliver or skip, reorder stops, copy orders to similar vehicles, route warnings.
Buying from depotsTap a depot to buy there, or choose the depot in the shop.
Money3-year finance sheet, loans and interest, running costs, inflation, bankruptcy after losing quarters.
Company, rating, goalsCompany value, 0 to 1000 performance rating with titles, 5 objectives with cash rewards.
Graphs and newsIncome, costs, cash, value, cargo, fleet graphs. News for town growth, production changes, rivals, offers to fund towns.
Breakdowns and servicingReliability drops, breakdowns happen, vehicles visit depots on their own.
Rival companiesUp to 3. Over 10 years each built 5 to 8 bus and truck routes and stayed solvent.
Save and loadManual save plus autosave every minute. Roads, tracks and vehicles come back.
Speed10 game years simulate in under a second. Smooth on the largest map.

Added in 1.6

Industries all over the mapEach industry goes to one of many good spots picked at random, away from others of its kind. How many of each you get varies from map to map. New ones can open anywhere.
Industries grow and shrinkSize 1 to 5. Mines, farms and wells expand after half a year of good service and shrink when left alone for two years. Factories grow with steady deliveries and make 10% more per size. Bigger sites get extra buildings.
Booms and bustsNow and then a mine finds a rich seam or runs thin, a farm has a bumper or poor harvest. Output jumps or drops by a third to a half.
Industry windowShows the size, a 12-month output chart, whether it is growing or falling, and what makes it grow.

Added in 1.5

Better touchPinch zooms around your fingers and pans at the same time. A quick swipe lets the map glide. The page itself no longer zooms by mistake.
Build anywhereWhile drawing road or track, pinch or move with two fingers and the line is kept. Drag to the screen edge and the map scrolls. Long lines wait with Build and Cancel.
Proper crossingsTrack crosses a road, and a road crosses track, only at a right angle and one tile at a time. No more track laid along a street.

Added in 1.4

Smoother drivingRoutes take the fewest bends. Buses, trucks and ships round corners, keep to their own lane and swing across when they turn round. Trains curve through bends.
Stops you can reachA vehicle's orders only list stops its depot's road, track or water connects to.
Every stop in townA new bus or truck calls at all the matching stops in the towns on its route. Older vehicles get a button to add them.
Names on the mapDocks and airports show their names, with a dot in the owner's colour.
No duplicate namesA second stop of the same kind in a town is named by direction, like Oakwood North Dock.

Added in 1.3: the new look

Light and shadowSunlight from the upper left. Slopes and cliffs are shaded, and buildings, trees and vehicles cast soft shadows.
Smoother landGrass colour blends across tiles, shores have sand, water is lighter in the shallows, and grid lines are off by default.
Living waterFoam along the shore, glints out on the water, and wakes behind ships.
New modelsHouses with windows, doors and proper roofs. Round and pointed trees that sway, palms in the tropics and lollipops in Toyland.
MotionSmoke from factories and steam trains, flares on refineries and rigs, and coins that pop up when you get paid.
Day and nightA day lasts five minutes. Windows, street lights, signals and headlights come on at dusk. Can be turned off in Settings.
Floating glass panelsThe map fills the screen. Windows float on wide screens and slide up from the bottom on phones; swipe down to close.
IconsAn icon dock for the building tools, grouped by road, rail, water, air and land, and an icon bar at the top.
Type, colour and dark modeInter font, numbers that keep their width, one yellow accent, and light or dark to match your device.
CardsMoney and Company open with summary cards and small charts. Vehicles show a coloured status.

Added in 1.2

Maps twice as bigSizes are now 128, 192, 256 and 320 tiles across. Towns, lakes and rivers grow with the map.
Docks checkedShips dock at both ends. Tap the shore or the water to build. The dock faces the biggest water and has a pier and crane. Ships warn you if a dock is on other water.
Rivals start laterThe first rival opens after two years, the next ones six months apart.
More industriesAbout 60% more at the start, more again on big maps, and new ones open four times as often.
Depots beside the lineBuild a depot or train depot on the land next to a road or track. A short piece joins it up.
Demolish houses and treesBulldoze clears trees and town buildings. Houses cost more and lower your rating with the town.

Added in 1.1 (were known limits)

Rivals at sea and in the airRivals with spare cash open oil tanker routes from rigs and, from 1955, plane routes between towns.
Block and path signalsBlock signals wait until the whole block is empty. Path signals only need the train's own route clear. Tap a signal to cycle the types.
Aircraft breakdownsWith breakdowns on, a plane can get an engine fault and makes an emergency landing at the nearest airport, then carries on.
Vehicles with depthVehicles get a roof and a front or back face, so they look solid from every direction.
Cloud savesSave to your Claude account under Saved games and load the game on another device.
Online racesShare the game, join the same race code and play the same map for 20 years with a live league table.

Phase 2: real railways

The biggest gap against Transport Tycoon Deluxe.

  1. 8
    Rail signals and single-track running
    Block and one-way signals with a Signal tool. Trains wait at red. Passing loops on single track. The signal logic is written and tested; it needs wiring into the game.
    LargeRail
  2. 9
    Long trains along the track
    Each wagon drawn on the track behind the locomotive, and a train taking up as many tiles as it is long.
    LargeRail
  3. 10
    Longer stations with platforms
    Build stations several tiles long and with more platforms. Bigger stations load more and hold more trains.
    MediumRail
  4. 11
    Level crossings
    Let track cross roads, with barriers that stop road vehicles when a train comes.
    MediumRail
  5. 12
    Electrified track
    Electric locomotives need electrified track, built over normal track for extra cost.
    SmallRail
